# Elvorie Sales Performance Dashboard

![Dashboard Preview](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/esramersa299-dev/Elvorie-Sales-Performance-Dashboard/main/Dashboard.png)

## 📌 Project Overview
This project is an interactive **Sales Performance Dashboard** built in **Power BI** to analyze sales performance across products, employees, customers, regions, and payment methods.
The dashboard enables users to monitor key business metrics, identify top-performing employees and customers, analyze payment and regional distribution, and explore sales drivers through interactive, drill-down visualizations.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective
The goal of this dashboard is to help business stakeholders answer questions such as:
- How is the business performing overall?
- How have sales changed over time?
- Which product category contributes the most revenue?
- Which employees are the top sales performers?
- Which customers generate the most revenue?
- Which payment methods do customers prefer?
- Which regions and cities drive the most sales?

---

## 💡 Key Insights
- **Phones** is the best-performing product category.
- **Aevo One 15** is the best-selling individual product.
- **Credit Card** is the most preferred payment method among customers.
- **Carlos Rivera** is the top-performing employee by total sales.
- **USA** region outperforms Europe in total revenue.

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### Executive KPIs
The dashboard provides four key performance indicators:
- Total Sales
- Total Orders
- Total Customers
- Average Order Value (AOV)

---

### Employee Leaderboard
A ranked bar chart showing total sales generated by each employee, making it easy to identify top and underperforming sales reps.

---

### Monthly Sales Trend
Tracks sales performance over time to identify trends, seasonality, and monitor business growth month over month.

---

### Top Revenue-Generating Customers
A ranked table highlighting the highest-value customers, using data bars for quick visual comparison.

---

### Revenue by Payment Method
A donut chart breaking down total sales by payment method (Credit Card, PayPal, Debit Card, Cash) to understand customer payment preferences.

---

### Sales Decomposition Tree
An interactive drill-down visual that explains Total Sales by Region and City, allowing users to explore what's driving performance at each level.

---

### Key Insights Panel
An auto-summarized narrative section translating the dashboard's data into clear business takeaways for non-technical stakeholders.

---

### Interactive Filters
The dashboard includes slicers that allow users to filter results by:
- Category
- Product
- Order Date (range slider)
- Region
- City

## 🗂 Data Model
Built as a star schema with `fact_sales` as the central fact table connected to seven dimension tables (customer, product, employee, store, payment, flags, and security).
